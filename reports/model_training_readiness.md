## 1. Problem Definition & Target Classes

The proposed machine learning task is to predict the physical land-use/combustion context of satellite active-fire observations across India:
- **Class 1 — `LIKELY_FOREST_CONTEXT`:** Wildland/forest canopy or understory fire in natural or reserved forest biomes (e.g., chir pine forests of Uttarakhand, sal forests of Chhattisgarh).
- **Class 2 — `LIKELY_AGRICULTURAL_CONTEXT`:** Seasonal post-harvest crop residue burning (e.g., Kharif paddy stubble burning in Punjab/Haryana, Rabi wheat residue in Madhya Pradesh/UP).
- **Class 3 — `OTHER_STATIC_THERMAL_SOURCE`:** Persistent non-vegetative thermal anomalies (e.g., oil refineries, steel furnaces, gas flares, cement kilns).
- **Class 4 — `UNCLASSIFIED`:** Insufficient spatial or temporal corroboration.

## 2. In-Depth Label Source Audit

| Candidate Label Source | Availability in Datasets | Nature of Labels | Independence & Leakage Risk |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **NASA MODIS `TYPE` Field** | Available only in standard archive (`M-C61`, 126,813 records); absent in NRT. | Internal algorithmic heuristic (`0`=presumed veg, `1`=volcano, `2`=static, `3`=offshore). | **High Leakage / Circularity.** `TYPE` is an automated proxy generated by the MOD14 algorithm itself, NOT field ground truth. |
| **NASA VIIRS Product** | Completely absent in `J1V-C2` standard & NRT products. | No type field supplied. | **N/A (Missing).** |
| **Forest Survey of India (FSI)** | Public portal alerts (`fsiforestfire.gov.in`). | Derived from SNPP-VIIRS and MODIS overpasses intersected with forest cover map. | **Verification Bias.** Using FSI as training labels means training a model to predict satellite detections using satellite detections. |
| **ISRO Bhuvan Agri Fire** | Seasonal portal layers (`bhuvan-app1.nrsc.gov.in`). | Derived from satellite thermal anomaly masks over crop mask layers. | **Circular Leakage.** Weak labels derived from the same sensor inputs. |
| **Field Incident Logs (Ground Truth)** | Not provided in FIRMS ZIP archives. | Physical on-the-ground firefighting incident reports with GPS perimeters. | **Completely Absent from supplied archives.** |

---

## 3. Decision Gate Criteria Assessment

1. **Existence of Independent Labels:** ❌ **FAILED.** Neither archive provides independent, ground-truthed labels.
2. **Quality & Quantity of Labels:** ❌ **FAILED.** Only algorithmic inferences exist; training a supervised model would create circular self-reinforcing bias.
3. **Class Balance & Representativeness:** ⚠️ **SKEWED.** Non-vegetative anomalies represent $<0.5\%$ of records.
4. **Leakage Safety:** ❌ **FAILED.** Any classifier trained on FIRMS radiometric features to predict FIRMS `TYPE` would merely fit internal lookup tables of the MOD14 algorithm.

---

## 4. Formal Scientific Decision

> [!IMPORTANT]
> ### DECISION: SUPERVISED ML CLASSIFIER DEFERRED
> In strict accordance with **Working Rule 7** (*"Do not train a classifier until you have verified the existence, semantics, quality, quantity and independence of labels"*), supervised model training is **RESPONSIBLY DEFERRED**.
> 
> Training a machine learning classifier without independent ground truth would generate fabricated precision scores and violate scientific standards.

  2. Agricultural harvest season windows (Kharif paddy stubble in Oct–Nov; Rabi wheat residue in Apr–May).
  3. Pre-monsoon dry forest fire seasons (Uttarakhand/Himachal chir pine peak in Mar–Jun; Central India sal forest peak in Feb–May).

- **Transparency:** Every classified observation outputs explicit `context_evidence` tokens explaining the reasoning to analysts.
