The Discontinuity Crisis

Why Raw Hotspot Concatenation Fails

The NASA fire archive is split across two fundamentally incompatible sensor generations.

1. The Spatial Resolution Gap

MODIS (1,000 m) vs VIIRS (375 m): Because of the 7× pixel area difference, VIIRS produces 7× to 23× more raw detections for the exact same physical fire front.

Blindly concatenating raw counts creates artificial multi-fold surges that distort long-term climate baselines.

2. Impending Constellation EOL

Suomi NPP Ingestion Ended: NASA FIRMS stopped new S-NPP data on Nov 02, 2026 (archive only).

Terra & Aqua EOL in 2027: Orbital drift and fuel exhaustion require immediate harmonization with NOAA-20/21 JPSS satellites.

💡 Without harmonization, 24 years of Earth observation data permanently fractures in 2027.

Our Innovation • Core USP

11-Stage Equal-Area Harmonization Engine

Mathematical resolution invariance through cylindrical equal-area projection and bipartite matching.

1. Equal-Area 1 km Binning

Projects all observations into equal-area cylindrical cells. Tracks unique_active_cells, eliminating 19.2% point density inflation.

2. Bipartite Overpass Matcher

Cross-sensor matching within 1,000 m and 60 min. Implements both Greedy Spatio-Temporal and Global Hungarian Assignment.

3. FRP Conservation Rule

Enforces zero duplicate energy counting. Collocated MODIS and VIIRS Fire Radiative Power (MW) values are never summed blindly.

Proven: Grid cell occupancy is resolution-invariant and completely neutralizes sensor pixel size differences.

Ethical Standards

Scientific Integrity & Ground Truth Attribution

Respecting the physics of satellite radiometry and avoiding unverified claims.

Hotspot ≠ Wildfire

Detections represent thermal anomalies (vegetation fires, stubble burns, gas flares, volcanoes). Never labeled "wildfire" without ground truth.

Physical Radiometry

Reports authentic dual-channel brightness in Kelvin (Channel 21/22 and Channel 31), maintaining raw radiometric fidelity.

Honest Classification

Strictly distinguishes Standard Reported FIRMS Classes from Near-Real-Time Inferred Context Classes.

Absence of detection ≠ absence of fire: Cloud cover and orbital gaps are explicitly accounted for.
